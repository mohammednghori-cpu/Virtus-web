# Virtus SEO deployment checklist

This version adds strong technical SEO foundations, but no code can guarantee a #1 ranking for every Google/Bing query.

## Included
- SEO title/description and keyword coverage
- Canonical URL
- OpenGraph metadata
- Robots directives
- XML sitemap covering product pages
- Organization/WebSite JSON-LD with official NAP details
- Internal links between services, solutions, products and contact
- Product pages with unique indexable URLs
- On-page portfolio terminology from the supplied company profile
- Consistent address and phone details in footer/contact

## After deployment
1. Verify /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml
2. Add virtusda.com to Google Search Console
3. Submit the sitemap
4. Add virtusda.com to Bing Webmaster Tools
5. Create/verify Google Business Profile with the exact business name, address and phone
6. Add real product images and product-specific datasheets/copy
7. Earn legitimate industry backlinks and directory/company-profile citations
8. Review Search Console queries monthly and add helpful pages around terms that actually bring impressions
